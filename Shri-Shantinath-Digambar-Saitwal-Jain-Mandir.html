1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Shri Shantinath Digambar Saitwal Jain Mandir Trust ("the Trust", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects the personal data of members, their families, and app users in connection with:

  • The member & chadhava management app (available on Android and as a web app at shrishantinath.in/mandir);
  • The Trust's official website (shrishantinath.in).

It is written to satisfy the disclosure requirements of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, the Information Technology Act, 2000, **Google Play Developer Policies**, and international privacy frameworks (including EU/UK GDPR and US state privacy laws). By using the app or providing data to the Trust office, you acknowledge this policy.

2. Personal data we collect

We only collect data connected to Trust membership, temple administration, and manual payment verification. We do not collect data for advertising, nor do we track user behavior across third-party websites or apps.

Category What we collect Source
Identity & Family Name, gender, date of birth, living/deceased status, and family relationships of members and household. Trust office at enrolment, or member input via the app.
Contact Details Mobile number, email address, postal address. Trust office or member profile updates.
Account & Authentication Login ID, salted cryptographic password hash (never stored in plain text), sign-in OTPs, and user role (member / accountant / administrator). System generated at account creation; OTPs generated during sign-in.
Membership & Financials Annual membership fee dues, payment and arrears history, receipt numbers, chadhava (temple offering) ledger entries. Trust office records linked to your member ID.
Payment References UPI transaction reference (UTR) numbers, payment timestamps, and verification status. We never collect or store card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank passwords. Submitted by you when reporting a manual payment.
Technical & Audit Logs Sign-in timestamps, administrative audit trail (who recorded a payment and when), IP address in secure server logs. Generated automatically by the app backend.

3. Why we collect it, and our legal basis

Under Section 7(a) of the DPDP Act and Article 6 of the EU GDPR, we process your personal data on the basis of your Consent (provided upon enrolment or signing in) and Legitimate Administration. We use your data strictly to:

  1. Maintain the Trust's member and family register;
  2. Calculate, collect, and record annual membership fees and dues;
  3. Maintain the chadhava ledger and issue official receipts;
  4. Authenticate your account and maintain security;
  5. Send dues reminders, receipts, and Trust event announcements;
  6. Fulfill legal, tax, and audit obligations required of registered public trusts in India.

4. App permissions

The Android app requests INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions strictly to communicate with the Trust's API servers.

The app does NOT request or access your:

  • Camera or Photo Gallery
  • Device Contacts or Address Book
  • GPS Location or Fine Location
  • Microphone, Storage, or SMS inbox

5. Who we share data with

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share it solely with verified service providers necessary to operate temple services:

  • Messaging Gateways: SMS, Email, and WhatsApp delivery providers for delivering OTPs, dues reminders, and receipts in Marathi, Hindi, and English.
  • Financial Institutions: Payment processors or your bank/UPI app when executing transactions. We store only the resulting reference number and approval status.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Database and hosting providers operating under strict contractual data security obligations.
  • Trust Officers: Trustees and accountants on a strict, role-based need-to-know administrative basis.
  • Legal Authorities: Government or judicial bodies only when mandated by law.

6. Where your data is stored & International Transfers

Your data is stored in secure database servers hosted in Mumbai, India.

If you use the app from outside India (such as international members or devotees), your personal data will be transferred to and processed in India, subject to the security standards defined in this policy.

7. How long we keep it & Account Deletion

Retention: Active membership and payment history are retained for as long as you remain a member, and up to 15 years post-membership to satisfy statutory tax, auditing, and financial reporting laws under Indian Trust legislation.

Account & Data Deletion Rights: In compliance with Google Play Developer Policy and global data protection laws, you have the right to request deletion of your app account and personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: Contact Trust administration directly through the app settings menu.
  • Web Deletion Request: You can submit a deletion request online at https://shrishantinath.in/delete-account or by emailing ssdsjmt@gmail.com.

Upon receiving your request, non-financial data will be permanently erased within 30 days. Required accounting records will be securely archived per statutory mandates.

8. How we protect it

We apply technical and organizational measures required under the Information Technology Act 2000 and DPDP Act 2023:

  • Passwords stored exclusively as salted cryptographic hashes (never readable plain text);
  • All network traffic protected via HTTPS / TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit;
  • Sensitive session keys stored in Android EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by Hardware Keystore;
  • Persistent idempotency keys to prevent duplicate submission risks over weak networks;
  • Strict role-based access control and account lockouts after repeated failed logins.

9. Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act and global data laws, you possess the right to:

  • Request a summary of personal data held about you and processing activities;
  • Correct or update inaccurate profile information;
  • Erase personal data when no longer needed for official trust purposes;
  • Withdraw consent at any time;
  • Nominate another person to exercise your privacy rights in case of death or incapacity (§14 of DPDP Act);
  • File complaints with our Grievance Officer or the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children's data

Family records may include minor children under 18. Such data is managed exclusively by an enrolled parent or legal guardian, which constitutes parental consent under Section 9 of the DPDP Act. We do not track, profile, or market to children.

11. Data breach notification

If a personal data breach ever occurs, we will notify affected users, CERT-In, and the Data Protection Board of India as required by applicable laws.
