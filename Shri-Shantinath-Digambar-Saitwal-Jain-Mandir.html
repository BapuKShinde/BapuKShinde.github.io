1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Shri Shantinath Digambar Saitwal Jain Mandir Trust ("the Trust", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects the personal data of members, their families, and app users, in connection with:

  • the member & chadhava management app, available on Android and as a web app at shrishantinath.in/mandir;
  • the Trust's website, shrishantinath.in.

It is written to meet the notice requirements of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 notified under it, and the disclosure requirements of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it. By using the app or providing your data to the Trust office, you acknowledge this policy.

2. Personal data we collect

We only collect data connected to Trust membership, temple administration and payments. We do not collect data for advertising, and the app requests no device permission beyond internet access (see §4).

3. Why we collect it, and our legal basis

Under the DPDP Act, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent (given when you or your family enrol with the Trust, or when you sign in to and use the app), and, for existing members, on the basis that the data was voluntarily provided for the purpose of an established relationship with the Trust (Section 7(a) of the DPDP Act). We use it only to:

  • maintain the Trust's member and family register;
  • calculate, collect and record annual membership fees and dues;
  • maintain the chadhava ledger and issue receipts;
  • authenticate you and keep your account secure;
  • send you dues reminders, receipts, and Trust news or event announcements;
  • meet the Trust's own statutory and accounting record-keeping obligations.

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting the Trust office (§12); this does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, and the Trust may still need to retain financial records it is legally required to keep.

4. App permissions

The Android app requests only the Internet permission, to communicate with the Trust's server. It does not request access to your camera, photos, contacts, location, storage, or SMS.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent, or use your personal data for advertising. We share it only where necessary to run the service, with:

  • SMS / email / WhatsApp delivery providers (for sending OTPs, dues reminders, receipts and notices in Marathi, Hindi and English), who process your mobile number, email address and message content solely to deliver that message;
  • Payment processors and your bank/UPI app, when you make a payment through the app — they process the payment itself; we only record the resulting reference and status;
  • Our hosting and database provider (§6), who stores data on our behalf under contract and does not use it for its own purposes;
  • Trust trustees and authorised office-bearers, for legitimate Trust administration, strictly on a need-to-know basis according to their role;
  • Government or judicial authorities, only where required by law.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in a database hosted in the Mumbai (India) region. We do not transfer your personal data outside India, other than to the limited extent a delivery provider in §5 processes it to send an SMS, email or WhatsApp message.

7. How long we keep it

Membership and dues records are kept for as long as you remain a member, and for up to 15 years afterward to preserve arrears and payment history for accounting and audit purposes, consistent with the Trust's record-keeping obligations. Account credentials are retained only while your account is active. You may ask us to erase your data earlier, subject to §9.

8. How we protect it

We apply reasonable security practices required under the IT Act, 2000 and the DPDP Act, including:

  • passwords stored only as salted cryptographic hashes, never in plain text;
  • all traffic between the app and our server encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS);
  • role-based access control, so a user can only see data relevant to their role;
  • account lock-out after repeated failed sign-in attempts;
  • an audit trail of who accessed or changed a record, and when.

No system is completely immune to risk; our obligations if this is ever compromised are set out in §11.

9. Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it has been processed;
  • correct, complete, or update your personal data;
  • erase your personal data, once it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for and we are not required by law to keep it;
  • withdraw consent at any time (§3);
  • nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity;
  • grievance redressal — raise a complaint with us (§12), and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these, contact the Trust office or Grievance Officer in §12. We will respond within the time limits prescribed under the DPDP Rules.

10. Children's data

Family records may include children under 18. Such records are added and managed by a parent or guardian who is themself an enrolled member — we treat this as the guardian's consent for their child's data under Section 9 of the DPDP Act. We do not knowingly use a child's data for behavioural monitoring, tracking, or targeted advertising. A parent or guardian may request correction or erasure of their child's data at any time (§9).

11. Data breach notification

If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals as required under the DPDP Rules, describing the nature of the breach, the data affected, and the steps being taken.
