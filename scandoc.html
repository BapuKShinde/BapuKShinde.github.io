🔒 Serverless & 100% Offline Document Security:
ScanDoc operates as a serverless application. We do not operate external databases or cloud servers for processing your documents. All scanning, image processing, OCR, and PDF generation happen 100% locally on your smartphone. Your scanned files never leave your device.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use my Service.

1. Document Privacy & Local On-Device Processing

ScanDoc is designed as a privacy-first, serverless utility suite. All features including camera document scanning, automatic edge detection, cropping, PDF creation, image-to-PDF conversion, merging, splitting, and file compression execute entirely on your local device.

  • Your scanned documents, images, and created PDFs are stored in your device's internal storage.
  • We do NOT collect, upload, transmit, or store your scanned documents on any server.

5. Cookies

This Service does not use these “cookies” explicitly. However, the app may use third-party code and libraries that use “cookies” to collect information and improve their services.
